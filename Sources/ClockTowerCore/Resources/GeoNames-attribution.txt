Clock Tower includes city data from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/).

Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
Additional region and country names:
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/admin1CodesASCII.txt
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/countryInfo.txt
Downloaded: 2026-11-04

GeoNames provides data "as is", without warranty of accuracy, timeliness, or completeness.

Changes: Clock Tower retains city IDs, names, aliases, regions, country codes,
population ranking, and IANA time zones; normalizes search text; formats display
names; uses "New York" for New York City; adds NYC and Nur-Sultan search aliases;
adds UK and USA country aliases; and explicitly maps Astana to Asia/Almaty.
Other source fields are omitted.
See catalog-source.txt for the retained city count and source-file SHA-256 digests.
The catalog is generated by Scripts/generate-city-catalog.py, not downloaded at runtime.
